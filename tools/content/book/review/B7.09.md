## 🌟 Learn more: buying a house (B7.09.8)

**B7.09.8.S01** · match_pairs · ⭐ ours
- Join the house words to their meanings
  - **tenant** ↔ a person who pays rent to live in a house = किरायेदार  (kiraayedaar)
  - **rent** ↔ money paid every month to live in a house = किराया  (kiraaya)
  - **loan** ↔ money borrowed from a bank = क़र्ज़  (karz)
  - **owner** ↔ the person a thing belongs to = मालिक  (maalik)
  - **lakh** ↔ one hundred thousand = लाख  (laakh)

**B7.09.8.S02** · choice · ⭐ ours
- Q: Mr. Choudhary had one and a half lakh and a loan of one lakh. How much is that? = चौधरी के पास डेढ़ लाख थे और एक लाख का क़र्ज़। कुल कितना?  (Choudhary ke paas dedh laakh the aur ek laakh ka karz. Kul kitna?)
- Options: two and a half lakh / three lakh / one lakh
- ✅ Answer: **two and a half lakh**
- ✍️ Exam answer: One and a half lakh plus one lakh is two and a half lakh, fifty thousand less than the price. = डेढ़ लाख और एक लाख मिलाकर ढाई लाख, जो क़ीमत से पचास हज़ार कम है।  (Dedh laakh aur ek laakh milaakar dhaai laakh, jo keemat se pachaas hazaar kam hai.)

**B7.09.8.S03** · fill_blank · ⭐ ours

- ✅ Answer: **rent** = हम हर महीने मकान मालिक को किराया देते हैं।  (Hum har mahine makaan maalik ko kiraaya dete hain.)
