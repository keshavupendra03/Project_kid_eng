## ✏️ Grammar in Use: plurals and short forms (B6.06.4)

**B6.06.4.S01** · learn_card · ⭐ ours
- Tinku (Hindi): बहुवचन के नियम: 1) ज़्यादातर में s: book → books। 2) s, sh, ch पर ख़त्म हो तो es: bus → buses। 3) व्यंजन + y हो तो ies: baby → babies। 4) स्वर (a, e, i, o, u) + y हो तो सिर्फ़ s: boy → boys।

## 🌟 Learn more: focus on the goal (B6.06.8)

**B6.06.8.S01** · match_pairs · ⭐ ours
- Join the archery words
  - **bow** ↔ a curved stick with a string to shoot arrows = धनुष  (dhanush)
  - **arrow** ↔ a thin stick with a sharp point = तीर  (teer)
  - **archer** ↔ a person who shoots with a bow = धनुर्धर  (dhanurdhar)
  - **aim** ↔ to point at a target = निशाना साधना  (nishaana saadhna)

**B6.06.8.S02** · choice · ⭐ ours
- Q: What is the lesson of this story? = इस कहानी की सीख क्या है?  (Is kahani ki seekh kya hai?)
- Options: Focus only on your goal to succeed. / Look at many things at once. / Birds live in trees.
- ✅ Answer: **Focus only on your goal to succeed.**
