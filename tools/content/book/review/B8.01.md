## 🌟 Learn more: sayings about hope (B8.01.8)

**B8.01.8.S01** · choice · ⭐ ours
- Q: ‘Every cloud has a silver lining’ means ... = इसका मतलब?  (Iska matlab?)
- Options: Something good comes even in bad times. / Clouds are made of silver. / It will rain today.
- ✅ Answer: **Something good comes even in bad times.**
- ✍️ Exam answer: Every cloud has a silver lining: even a bad situation has something good in it. = हर बादल में चाँदी की लकीर होती है: बुरे समय में भी कुछ अच्छा छिपा होता है।  (Har baadal mein chaandi ki lakeer hoti hai: bure samay mein bhi kuchh achha chhipa hota hai.)

**B8.01.8.S02** · choice · ⭐ ours
- Q: ‘Practice makes perfect’ means ... = इसका मतलब?  (Iska matlab?)
- Options: Doing something again and again makes you good at it. / You should never practise. / Only perfect people practise.
- ✅ Answer: **Doing something again and again makes you good at it.**

**B8.01.8.S03** · choice · ⭐ ours
- Q: ‘Failure is the stepping stone to success’ means ... = इसका मतलब?  (Iska matlab?)
- Options: We learn from failure and then succeed. / Failure is a kind of stone. / Never try again.
- ✅ Answer: **We learn from failure and then succeed.**
