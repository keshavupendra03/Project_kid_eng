**B8.05.1.S02** · learn_word · ⭐ ours
- **miser** = कंजूस (kanjoos) — a person who hates to spend money
- Example: The miser never bought anything. = कंजूस ने कभी कुछ नहीं ख़रीदा।  (Kanjoos ne kabhi kuchh nahi khareeda.)

**B8.05.1.S03** · learn_word · ⭐ ours
- **treasure** = ख़ज़ाना (khazaana) — a collection of valuable things
- Example: He hid his treasure in a hole. = उसने अपना ख़ज़ाना गड्ढे में छिपाया।  (Usne apna khazaana gaddhe mein chhipaaya.)

**B8.05.1.S04** · learn_word · ⭐ ours
- **knelt** = घुटनों के बल बैठा (ghutnon ke bal baitha) — went down on the knees
- Example: He knelt by the hole. = वह गड्ढे के पास घुटनों के बल बैठा।  (Woh gaddhe ke paas ghutnon ke bal baitha.)

**B8.05.1.S05** · learn_word · ⭐ ours
- **clatter** = शोर-शराबा (shor-sharaaba) — a loud noise
- Example: He made a terrible clatter. = उसने बहुत शोर मचाया।  (Usne bahut shor machaaya.)
