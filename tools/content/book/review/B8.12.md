## 🔄 Let's Learn: active and passive voice (B8.12.4)

**B8.12.4.S01** · learn_card · ⭐ ours
- Tinku (Hindi): Active: करने वाला पहले: The hunter killed the tiger। Passive: जिस पर काम हुआ वह पहले: The tiger was killed by the hunter। Object कर्ता बनता है, क्रिया be + तीसरा रूप, और by + करने वाला।
- Tinku (English): Active: the doer comes first: The hunter killed the tiger. Passive: the receiver comes first: The tiger was killed by the hunter. The object becomes the subject, the verb is be + 3rd form, then by + the doer.
