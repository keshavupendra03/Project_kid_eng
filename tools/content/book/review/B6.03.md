**B6.03.1.S02** · learn_word · ⭐ ours
- **ruler** = शासक, राजा (shaasak, raja) — a person who rules a country

**B6.03.1.S03** · learn_word · ⭐ ours

- Example: The king won the battle. = राजा ने युद्ध जीता।  (Raja ne yuddh jeeta.)

**B6.03.1.S04** · learn_word · ⭐ ours
- **cave** = गुफ़ा (gufaa) — a big hole in the side of a hill
- Example: Bats live in the cave. = चमगादड़ गुफ़ा में रहते हैं।  (Chamgaadad gufaa mein rehte hain.)

**B6.03.1.S05** · learn_word · ⭐ ours
- **succeed** = सफल होना (safal hona) — to do what you tried to do

**B6.03.1.S06** · learn_word · ⭐ ours
- **confidence** = आत्मविश्वास (aatmavishwaas) — a feeling that you can do something
- Example: She spoke with confidence. = उसने आत्मविश्वास से बात की।  (Usne aatmavishwaas se baat ki.)

**B6.03.1.S07** · learn_word · ⭐ ours
- **defeat** = हराना (haraana) — to win against someone
- Example: Our team defeated the other team. = हमारी टीम ने दूसरी टीम को हराया।  (Hamari team ne doosri team ko haraaya.)
