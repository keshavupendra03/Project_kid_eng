## ✏️ Grammar in Use: joining with ‘who’ (B7.02.4)

**B7.02.4.S01** · learn_card · ⭐ ours
- Tinku (Hindi): दो वाक्यों में एक ही इंसान हो, तो उन्हें ‘who’ से जोड़ सकते हैं। ‘who’ का मतलब है ‘जो’। The girl is Sarita. The girl is wearing a red sweater. → The girl who is wearing a red sweater is Sarita.
- Tinku (English): When two sentences are about the same person, join them with ‘who’. The girl is Sarita. The girl is wearing a red sweater. → The girl who is wearing a red sweater is Sarita.

**B7.02.4.S02** · learn_card · ⭐ ours
- Tinku (Hindi): याद रखो: ‘who’ लोगों के लिए है। जानवरों और चीज़ों के लिए ‘which’ लगाते हैं: The dog which barks is mine.
- Tinku (English): Remember: ‘who’ is for people. For animals and things we use ‘which’: The dog which barks is mine.

**B7.02.4.S03** · build_sentence · 📕 BOOK
- Task (Hindi): जोड़ो: The boy is wearing a blue cap. + The boy is the tallest boy in the class.
