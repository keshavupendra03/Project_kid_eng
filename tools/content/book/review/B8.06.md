## ❔ Let's Learn (A, B): making questions (B8.06.4)

**B8.06.4.S01** · learn_card · ⭐ ours
- Tinku (Hindi): Yes/No सवाल सहायक क्रिया (is, are, does, did, will, can, has) से शुरू होता है: Was he playing cricket? Wh-सवाल: सवाल शब्द + सहायक + कर्ता + क्रिया: Where do you live?
- Tinku (English): A Yes/No question begins with a helping verb: Was he playing cricket? A Wh-question: question word + helping verb + subject + verb: Where do you live?
